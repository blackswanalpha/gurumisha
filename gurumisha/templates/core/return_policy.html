Return Policy

Your satisfaction is our priority. Learn about our comprehensive return and refund policies.

Return Conditions Return Process

Our Return Promise

We stand behind every vehicle and part we sell. Our return policy is designed to protect your investment and ensure your satisfaction.

7-Day Return Window

Return eligible items within 7 days of delivery for a full refund, subject to our return conditions.

Quality Guarantee

All vehicles undergo thorough inspection. If quality issues are discovered, we'll make it right.

Full Refund

Eligible returns receive 100% refund of the purchase price, processed within 5-7 business days.

Return Conditions

Please review these conditions to ensure your return meets our policy requirements.

Vehicle Returns

Eligible for Return:

  • Significant undisclosed mechanical issues
  • Misrepresented vehicle condition
  • Documentation discrepancies
  • Failed independent inspection

Not Eligible:

  • Change of mind after 7 days
  • Normal wear and tear
  • Damage after delivery
  • Modifications made to vehicle

Spare Parts Returns

Eligible for Return:

  • Defective or damaged parts
  • Wrong part shipped
  • Unopened original packaging
  • Within 30 days of purchase

Not Eligible:

  • Used or installed parts
  • Custom or special order items
  • Electrical parts (unless defective)
  • Fluids and consumables

Return Process

Follow these simple steps to initiate and complete your return request.

1

Contact Support

Contact our customer service team within the return window to initiate your return request.

Required: Order number, reason for return, photos (if applicable)
2

Return Authorization

Receive return authorization number (RMA) and detailed return instructions via email.

Timeline: Within 24 hours of request
3

Prepare Return

Package item securely with all original accessories, documentation, and RMA number.

Include: Original packaging, manuals, accessories, purchase receipt
4

Ship or Schedule Pickup

Ship to our returns center or schedule pickup for vehicles. We'll provide shipping labels for eligible returns.

Vehicles: Pickup arranged | Parts: Ship to returns center
5

Inspection & Processing

Our team inspects the returned item and processes your refund upon approval.

Timeline: 3-5 business days after receipt
6

Refund Issued

Refund processed to your original payment method with confirmation email sent.

Timeline: 5-7 business days to appear in account

Refund Information

Important details about how refunds are processed and what to expect.

Refund Timeline

  • M-Pesa: 1-2 hours
  • Bank transfer: 3-5 days
  • Credit card: 5-7 days
  • PayPal: 3-5 days

Refund Method

Refunds are processed to your original payment method.

  • Same payment method used
  • Full purchase amount
  • Shipping costs included

Partial Refunds

Some situations may result in partial refunds:

  • Missing accessories
  • Damaged packaging
  • Return shipping costs
